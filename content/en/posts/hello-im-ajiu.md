## Who I Am

Hi, I'm Ajiu, a test engineer.

I've been working in production-line testing for a while, currently focused on test R&D — FCT/IGBT testing. I've spent my career around the word "test": testing boards, modules, data, and occasionally my own patience.

This blog is called 404-found.uk, a pun: 404 usually means "not found" — but here, it found you.

## What I'm Doing

- **Work**: FCT/IGBT testing, host-PC automation, production-line test R&D
- **Learning**: Python, host-PC development, automated testing — grinding up from the basics
- **Life**: tinkering with gadgets, anime, music, and the occasional hardware fix — like resoldering a loose crystal oscillator on a USB NIC with my own hands

## Why Blog

Because I've realized that thinking something through and writing it clearly are two different things.

Writing is a review for myself; publishing is a reference for fellow travelers. I don't write fast, and I don't write much, but every post is thought through before I put it down — no chasing update frequency, no writing things I haven't thought through.
